Pudding Tort

Ingredients: 

1 8 oz package of cream cheese 

1 8 oz tub of cool whip 

A pack of powdered sugar 

A bag of chocolate chips. 

 

Step one: mix 8 oz. Of crease cheese with 1 cup of powdered sugar, then fold in 1.5 cups of cool whip. 

Step two: spread mixture onto premade shortbread crust and chill in the refrigerator while doing step three. 

Step three: mix 2 packs of devil's food pudding with 3 cups of milk until smooth. 

Step four: spread pudding mixture over the first layer. 

Step five: spread remaining cool whip on top of both layers. 

Step six: sprinkle mini chocolate chips on top as desired 

Step seven: chill in refrigerator for 4 hours prior to serving.
