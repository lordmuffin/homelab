One Dish Chicken & Rice Bake 

Campbell&apos;s One Dish Chicken & Rice Bake Recipe

Chicken and rice paired with a creamy mushroom sauce bake together for a delicious one-dish meal that's easy to make and easy to clean up. 

What You'll Need 

1 can (10 3/4 ounces) Campbell's® Condensed Cream of Mushroom Soup (Regular or 98% Fat Free) 

1 cup water 

3/4 cup uncooked regular long-grain white rice 

1/4 teaspoon paprika 

1/8 teaspoon ground black pepper 

4 boneless chicken breast halves - add cajun and season salt 

Corn 

 

How to Make It 

12 

Stir the soup, water, rice, paprika and black pepper in a 2-quart shallow baking dish. Top with the chicken. Season with additional paprika and black pepper. Cover the baking dish. 

Bake at 375°F. for 45 minutes or until the chicken is cooked through and the rice is tender.  Let stand for 10 minutes.  Stir the rice before serving. 

Serves 

Recipe Note: For creamier rice, increase the water to 1 1/3 cups. 

Serving Suggestion: Serve with a steamed vegetable blend and crusty whole wheat rolls. For dessert serve fresh apple slices and store-bought caramel sauce for dipping. 

Ingredient Note: We develop our recipes using a 4-to 5-ounce skinless, boneless chicken breast half per serving. However, there are a range of sizes available in-store, from the butcher counter to the meat case and the freezer section. Use whichever you prefer- just follow the recipe as written above for the best result. If you're using larger chicken breasts they may require a little longer cooking time.
