Watergate Cake 
Cake 

1 PKG Yellow or White Cake Mix 

1 PKG Pistachio instant pudding 

1 cup oil 

3 Eggs 

1 cup 7-up 

 

Topping 

9oz cool whip 

1 cup milk 

1 PKG pistachio instant pudding 

 

 

Mix cake ingredients.  Pour into greased and floured 9x3 pan.  Bake at 350 for 35-45 MIN. Cool. Mix topping Ingredients, spread over cooled cake.  Refrigerate.
