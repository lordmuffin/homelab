Plan ahead, crust needs to refrigerate for 30 minutes. 

 

Crust: 

2 1/2 cups flour 

1 tbsp sugar 

1 tsp salt 

1 cup butter - actual butter, not margarine or other fake crap 

1 egg yolk plus water to equal 2/3 cup (reserve egg white) 

 

Filling: 

6-8 cups chopped rhubarb 

3/4 sugar 

4 tbsp minute tapioca 

1/4 tsp cinnamon  

 

Combine flour, sugar and salt; cut in butter.  Add yolk mixture bringing together into a ball.  I roll it around on a floured surface, adding enough flour to form a ball.  Divide into two balls and wrap each in plastic wrap; refrigerate 30 minutes.   

 

Combine sugar, tapioca and cinnamon. 

 

Roll one crust into rectangle to fit 11x14 jelly roll pan.  Pour chopped rhubarb evenly on crust.  Sprinkle with sugar mixture.  Roll second crust and place over filling, trimming edges to be even with bottom crust and edge of pan.  DO NOT seal edges.  Slice vent holes in top.  Brush with egg white. 

 

Bake 10 minutes at 450, then reduce temp to 350 and bake for 20 minutes.  Cool.  Drizzle with icing. 

 

Icing: 

1 cup powdered sugar to 2-4 tsp milk.  This is guesstimate, you may need to adjust as you mix, but be careful of too much milk at once.  It should be thin, but not runny.  I place in a sandwich bag, then clip one small corner to drizzle.  A measuring cup with spout also will work, it just pours the icing into larger streaks.
