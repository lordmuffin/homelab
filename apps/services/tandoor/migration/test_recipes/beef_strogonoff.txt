Beef Strogonoff

1lb ground beef 

2 tbsp flour 

1/2 tsp salt 

1 tsp garlic powder 

1/4 tsp pepper 

1/2 can of chicken broth 

Half stick salted butter 

1 can of Cream of chicken soup 

1 cup sour cream - or to taste 

2 cups cooked noodles 

 

Brown ground beef in - drain.  Stir in flour, salt, garlic, pepper, and broth.  Cook 5 min, stirring constantly.  Add in soup, simmer for 10 min.  Stir in sour cream to taste and heat thoroughly.  Serve over noodles.
