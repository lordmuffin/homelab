Grammie Sugar Cookies

Dry: 

3 Cup Flour 

1 cup sugar 

1 1/2 tsp. Baking powder 

1/ 2 tsp. Salt 

Addin: 

1 cup soft butter 

1 slightly beaten egg 

3 tablespoons cream 

1 teaspoon vanilla 

 

 

Icing:  

2 Tablespoons Butter 

2 Cups powdered sugar 

2-4 Teaspoon vanilla 

2 tbs heavy cream 

 

 

Blend wet and dry ingredients separately, then together. 

 

Can refrig dough if soft. 

 

Roll out with flour if needed to 1/2" or less.  Cut into desired shapes 

Bake at 350 8 - 10 min until lightly brown, do not grease pan. 

 

Icing:  

Mix ingredients. Enough to frost one batch of cookies.
