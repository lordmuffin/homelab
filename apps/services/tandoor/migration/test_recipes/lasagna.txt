1 pkg ground turkey (1.25lb, use The Turkey Store brand) 

3 - 15 oz cans tomatoes - any combo of chopped/diced with or without flavorings - I use some sort of flavored ones, it varies 

6 - 12 oz tomato paste 

1 tsp Tarragon 

1 1/2 tsp Basil 

1 1/2 tsp Rosemary 

1 1/2 - 2 Tbls Sugar 

 

Lasagana noodles - NOT the no bake kind 

2 cups Mozzarella cheese 

12 oz Cottage Cheese 

 

Cook noodles, rinse with cold water.  Brown meat, drain any excess water.  Add tomatoes, spices, and simmer for 20 minutes.  Place small amount of sauce in the bottom of a 9x13 pan, just enough to coat so noodles don't stick.  Layer noodles, sauce, Mozzarella, and Cottage Cheese.  3-4 layers.  Cover with foil.  Bake at 350 for 40-50 minutes, remove cover for last 10 minutes of baking for crispy cheese, otherwise leave covered.  The more layers, the longer the bake time. 

 

Can be put together ahead of time and refrigerated.  Let set at room temp 30-45 minutes before baking.  Baking time will probably be  increased by 10 minutes.
