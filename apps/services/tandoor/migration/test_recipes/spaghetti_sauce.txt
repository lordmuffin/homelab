1/2 Onion Chopped 

2 tablespoons olive oil 

2-3 Garlic Cloves, Minced 

2 15oz Diced Tomatoes cans 

1 6oz tomato paste can 

1/8tsp garlic powder 

1tsp salt 

1/2tsp pepper 

1 1/2tsp oregano 

1tsp Worcestershire sauce 

Brown Sugar to taste 

 

Directions 

Food Processor chop onion and garlic maybe use oil for this? 

In a large skillet over medium heat, saute the minced garlic and onion until garlic browns.  Add remaining ingredients with water to the correct consistency. Cover; let sauce simmer over low heat for 45-60 minutes.  Put sauce in refrigerator for 24 hours. 

Cook 1lb of meat in skillet while heating sauce in saucepan.  Once meat is cooked add to sauce, and simmer for at least 20 minutes.  Serve over cooked pasta.
